A few things to know.

How are games priced?

Where a matching market reference is available, the listed USD price equals that reference plus 5%, rounded to the nearest cent. Cases and boxes are priced using complete-in-box references by the seller’s instruction; actual manuals, contents, authenticity, and working condition still need confirmation. Unmatched items show “Price under review.” These are dated reference estimates, not guaranteed appraisals.

How does shipping work?

Domestic orders are intended to ship through USPS Ground Advantage or Priority Mail; international orders through eligible USPS international services. Shipping is quoted separately after the destination, packed weight, and dimensions are known. International availability varies by destination, and customs charges may apply.
